# Contributing

Spatial Forming is an early concept. The most valuable contributions right now are not code, but **knowledge and honest criticism**.

## Most wanted

1. **Prior art.** Projects, papers or patents that already combine hand-based spatial modelling with a personal learning layer or an open movement/function library.
2. **Measurements.** Real precision data of camera-based hand tracking at a desk (see Experiment 0 in [docs/TECHNICAL_REVIEW.md](docs/TECHNICAL_REVIEW.md#smallest-experiments)).
3. **Architecture critique.** Where does the proposed separation of raw movement, operations log, personal profile and public library break?

## How

- **Issues** for concrete points: a missed project, an error, a contradiction.
- **Discussions** for open questions and ideas.

Please label your input the same way the documents do: requirement, proposal, known technology, assumption, open question. Unverified claims are welcome, as long as they are marked as such.

## Ground rules

- Critical objections are explicitly welcome.
- Known functions are not presented as new inventions.
- The original concept ([docs/CONCEPT.md](docs/CONCEPT.md)) is not changed silently. Proposals that change the vision go into an issue first.
